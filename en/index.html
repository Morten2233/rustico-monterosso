High above Monterosso lies this fully renovated rustico made of two small houses, with a fantastic view over the bay of Monterosso.

Completely secluded, above a former monastery that is now a restaurant and hotel.

The two houses offer about 50 m² of living space with two rooms and a bathroom, plus an open, fully equipped, high-quality kitchen with seating under a weatherproof pergola.

The high-quality furniture and the professional outdoor kitchen are included in the price. South-facing with an unobstructed view over the bay.
